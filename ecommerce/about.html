How LuxeNest Started

Founded in 2018 with a single vision — to make finding your perfect home as effortless as possible. We saw a broken real estate market filled with outdated listings and inaccessible expertise, and decided to change it.

Today, LuxeNest is one of the fastest-growing real estate platforms serving buyers, sellers, and renters across the United States and Canada. Our team of 200+ licensed agents combined with our proprietary AI chatbot gives every client a premium, personalized experience.

We are not just a listing platform — we are your dedicated partner in every step of your real estate journey.
